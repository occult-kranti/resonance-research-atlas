**Decision:** No blocking dimensional or evidence-scope error in the proposed research framework. Two wording/compartment refinements were sent to the historical agent; that agent owns edits to its files.

1. **HN-C optical scope:** Define εr explicitly as εparticle/εmedium at the same wavelength and fixed host. The invariant a³q family is a valid toy counterexample when its contrast values are admissible, not proof that every scaled pair corresponds to real gold with causal dispersion. Absorption alone does not invalidate a complex-permittivity quasistatic dipole model. Replace the blanket “model failure at absorption” phrase with omitted absorption/readout channel, missing radiative correction or departure from the size/shape/interaction regime. Additional wavelengths help only when independent dispersion information is supplied.
2. **HN-D mass compartments:** State that Mp is suspended particulate elemental mass, including clusters, and Mw is deposited/collected particulate mass. This makes sedimentation Mp→Mw disjoint and prevents double counting; total tracked element is Mp+Md+Mw. Association changes suspended entity count without creating elemental mass.
