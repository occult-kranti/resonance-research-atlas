# Historical hypothesis measurement plates

Created 2026-09-26. These six repo-native SVGs illustrate the six proposals in `historical-hypotheses.md`. They are proposed measurement logic, not built apparatus, reconstructed historical schematics or empirical results. They do not determine which five adaptive computation contracts the advisor selects.

Each plate names inputs, observables and a rival/control. Solid arrows indicate model or measurement dependence; dashed arrows indicate candidate processes or proposed controls. The force panel uses vector directions only for its stated support, weight and streaming-drag components. No generated raster artwork is used.

| Proposal | SVG | What the plate makes explicit |
|---|---|---|
| HN-A | [Loss per cycle versus power](../../assets/diagrams/history-nano-a-cycle-power.svg) | Fixed-parameter Debye shapes, distinct units, calibrated sample field, and a thermal/calibration control. The displayed curves are analytic model functions. |
| HN-B | [Particle count and mean volume](../../assets/diagrams/history-nano-b-particle-count.svg) | Equal-number synthetic 40/60 nm spheres, exact third moment, 12% count bias and a monodisperse control. The calculation reproduces an existing literature result; these diameters are not a validated magnetic-particle regime. |
| HN-C | [Color inference and independent measurement](../../assets/diagrams/history-nano-c-color-inference.svg) | Sample → optical model → instrument projection, a synthetic radius/contrast ambiguity and independent assay. Color does not uniquely name a historical compound. |
| HN-D | [Matter pathways and distinct mass pools](../../assets/diagrams/history-nano-d-matter-process.svg) | Dispersed particles/clusters within suspended elemental mass, plus separate dissolved and deposited pools; candidate aggregation/dissolution/settling and joint-observable controls. No preparation recipe. |
| HN-E | [Harmonic information and calibration](../../assets/diagrams/history-nano-e-harmonic-information.svg) | System-matrix units, duplicate-row versus independent-row logic, correlated-noise/calibration checks and patent status. Not an implementation of the patent. |
| HN-F | [Force and energy boundaries](../../assets/diagrams/history-nano-f-force-energy.svg) | Particle/support forces, environment controls, energy storage and complete input/output integration. Supported motion alone does not establish gravity cancellation. |

Source IDs NH01–NH22 resolve in `sources-history.json` from the accompanying research packet. Newton, Faraday and Tesla provide selected historical questions; modern forward models and equations are separately attributed. Their names do not imply endorsement, a complete corpus review or modern nanoparticle knowledge.
