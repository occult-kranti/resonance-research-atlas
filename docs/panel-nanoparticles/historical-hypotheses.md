It is not a certain prediction of a physical planetary catastrophe, a particle-frequency calculation or an experiment deadline.

Jung/Pauli are a cultural-association lens: preserve a symbol's document context and prevent a compelling analogy from silently becoming a chemical identity. The ETH exhibit was read; no complete correspondence or individual letter was newly collated (NH13). Penrose's 1996 abstract supplies a specifically defined mass-superposition lifetime proposal, not a generic mechanism coupling consciousness to colloids (NH14). Feynman's 1974 methods discussion motivates publishing alternatives, failed tests and new predictions (NH15).

## Six operational hypothesis candidates

These are modern proposals. “Novelty” means the proposed contribution relative to the nearest sources checked here, not a worldwide priority claim. All start with computational or recorded-data work; any later physical study would require a separate apparatus and material assessment.

### HN-A — The frequency maximizing loss per cycle need not maximize heating per time

**Status:** selected as a candidate for the first new loop by the advisor; result belongs in that loop's review files, not this proposal.

**Model and rival:** For a weak-drive, fixed-parameter Debye model, set x = 2πfτ and χ″ = χ₀x/(1+x²). With peak H in A/m, loss per cycle per volume is w = πμ₀H²χ″ in J/m³; average power density is p = fw in W/m³. A peak of χ″ at x = 1 competes with the claim that the same frequency maximizes p at fixed H. Changes in H(f), τ(T), interactions and instrument response are distinct rival explanations for a measured maximum.

**Observable/control:** Calculate w(f) and p(f) separately with stated normalization; compare fixed-H with a separately specified drive-resource constraint. Add an inert thermal blank and synthetic calibration drift before interpreting calorimetric slopes. Report f in Hz, τ in s and the measured field at the sample rather than a generator dial.

**Falsifier:** A numerical curve inconsistent with the analytic derivative or p = fw fails the model implementation. Data outside the fixed-parameter prediction challenge those assumptions; they do not by themselves establish a nonthermal effect. The model has no clinical outcome variable.

**Nearest checked source / novelty:** Rosensweig 2002 §§2–3, Eqs.2,6–9 (NH22). The proposed contribution is an auditable objective/constraint comparison, not discovery of magnetic heating. Tesla's source/load lens is historical motivation only.

### HN-B — Particle count inferred from an average diameter has a predictable distribution bias

**Model and rival:** For homogeneous spherical particles with known density ρ (kg/m³), mass concentration Cₘ (kg/m³ of suspension), and a number-weighted diameter distribution d in m, n = Cₘ/[ρ(π/6)E(d³)] in m⁻³. Substituting E(d)³ asserts an effectively monodisperse volume. This competes with the distribution-aware mass sum.

**Observable/control:** Use finite positive supports and exact rational moments, then perturb density and size calibration separately. Equal counts at 40 and 60 nm have μ = 50 nm, CV = 0.2, E(d³) = 140000 nm³ and μ³ = 125000 nm³. Thus the mean-diameter estimator is 1.12 times the correct count. Verify by summing individual particle masses. A monodisperse population is the zero-bias control.

**Falsifier:** Failure of the exact mass sum or n-estimator ratio invalidates the implementation. A real discrepancy may instead indicate wrong density, non-sphericity, aggregation, missing small particles or intensity-weighted sizes. State which population weighting was measured.

**Nearest checked source / novelty:** Farkas et al. 2025, PDF pp.4 and 7, Eqs.1–2 and5 (NH17). The 12% example is deliberately a reproduction of a known effect using a positive two-point population. An optional exact extension uses E(d³)/μ³ = 1 + 3CV² + γ₁CV³, with skewness defined from the same number-weighted population. No new physical law is claimed. Newton's separate mass and appearance records motivate independent bookkeeping, not attribution of this formula to him.

### HN-C — Optical strength can leave particle size and material contrast non-identifiable

**Model and rival:** Begin with an explicitly synthetic quasistatic sphere model, not colored-gold data: define dimensionless contrast q = (εᵣ−1)/(εᵣ+2) and reduced polarizability a³q, with radius a in m and relative permittivity εᵣ = εparticle/εmedium at the same fixed medium and frequency. Dipole scattering depends on its squared magnitude at a fixed wavelength. The transformation a → sa, q → q/s³ preserves that reduced response. Unknown concentration supplies another confounder in ensemble measurements. A fitted single radius competes with a family of radius/contrast pairs.

**Observable/control:** Record wavelength-resolved extinction/scattering and illumination, path length, medium and detector response. In the toy model compare a known-contrast control, an independent geometric-size measurement, and an additional wavelength that is genuinely sensitive to different dispersion. Two copies of the same channel are a negative information-gain control. A claimed color image is only an instrument projection of a spectrum.

**Falsifier:** If a proposed second channel has proportional sensitivity throughout the stated parameter domain, it does not remove the ambiguity. Large size parameter, insufficient radiative correction, omitted absorption/readout channels, shape anisotropy or interacting clusters can invalidate the chosen forward/readout model. Complex-permittivity dipole models can include absorption. The scaled contrast must also remain compatible with the declared material domain; it need not obey a real material’s dispersion or passivity across other frequencies. A scale-invariant toy model cannot demonstrate actual gold-plasmon spectra.

**Nearest checked source / novelty:** Newton's conditional conjecture (NH01), Faraday's alternative interpretations (NH04), Mie's bibliographic foundation (NH07; full derivation not read), and NIST's present multi-attribute measurement project (NH19). The proposed contribution is a transparent counterexample plus an independent-channel design, not a new claim that nanomaterial colors are nonunique. The exact optical forward equations must receive modern physics review before a loop freezes them.

### HN-D — A color change can reflect aggregation, dissolution or medium change

**Model and rival:** Use a conceptual matter-balance model with suspended particulate elemental mass Mₚ (including suspended clusters), dissolved mass M_d and separately collected/deposited particulate elemental mass M_w (kg), plus suspended entity count N and a distribution of sizes. Aggregation can reduce N while preserving total particulate mass; dissolution transfers mass between Mₚ and M_d; sedimentation transfers suspended mass to M_w; a changed optical environment may alter a spectrum without any of those transfers. Surface oxidation or a new phase is an additional chemistry hypothesis, requiring a phase/speciation measurement.

**Observable/control:** Simulate each mechanism separately before mixtures. Observe spectral shape, an independently calibrated count/size distribution, dissolved fraction and total elemental recovery. A medium-only blank and a matched path-length control probe optical confounds. Do not infer a named alloy, oxidation state or alchemical identity from RGB alone.

**Falsifier:** A mechanism is rejected within the measurement model when its predicted mass/count/signature cannot jointly match the observations within stated uncertainty. Conservation failure first prompts an unmeasured compartment or calibration audit, not transmutation. Historical substances and modern particles need not share the same kinetics.

**Nearest checked source / novelty:** Faraday pp.420 and429 (NH04–NH05), Newton's selected separated fractions (NH02), and the NIST count paper (NH17). The proposed contribution is a joint-observable diagnostic matrix for a historical ambiguity; no claim to a first aggregation assay, complete alchemical decipherment or tested reaction network.

### HN-E — More harmonic channels help inversion only when their calibrated information is independent

**Model and rival:** In U = Ac + e, U is a calibrated voltage vector, c is a concentration vector in a declared unit, A is volts per concentration, and e includes correlated noise and feed-through. Multiple harmonics or sidebands may distinguish columns of A, or may repeat an existing row/noise mode. Regularization can stabilize a chosen answer without making the inverse unique.

**Observable/control:** Compare nullity, a noise-whitened sensitivity spectrum, held-out predictive error and calibrated covariance. Use a truly independent added row, a duplicate-row control, and a miscalibrated row with optimistic nominal rank. Hold the acquisition-time or noise budget fixed when comparing devices.

**Falsifier:** If added channels do not improve identifiability or held-out performance at equal resources, the information-gain claim fails for this model. Compact SVD of a wide matrix can omit its domain-kernel zeros; report columns minus rank. This release must not silently rerun the earlier R3 example and call it a new physical discovery.

**Nearest checked source / novelty:** CN120405529B's proposed system matrix and harmonic sidebands (NH20). The new question, if selected, is calibration/correlated-noise sensitivity in a declared synthetic system, not a recreation or empirical validation of that patent.

### HN-F — Powered suspension and stored-energy release can imitate stronger claims when the boundary is incomplete

**Model and rival:** Keep two observables distinct: a force balance for a suspended particle, and a complete energy balance for its source/detector. For force, fit conventional field force, drag/streaming, buoyancy and gravity in N. For energy, include source input, radiation/heat entering, storage change and output in J. A load that rises can be supported by ordinary applied force; a discharge pulse can exceed instantaneous input power by releasing previously stored energy.

**Observable/control:** First use a simulated balance with hidden nuisance forces/initial storage deliberately toggled. In a proposed future instrument, source-off, field reversal, orientation, medium-pressure/flow and matched-temperature dummy controls test specific competing forces; measure recoil on the support where relevant. Duration and bandwidth are part of the energy integration. An unexplained residual would motivate a bounded follow-up, not automatically identify antigravity or free energy.

**Falsifier:** Reject an identified conventional model only if residuals survive calibrated uncertainty and the control that targets its particular mechanism. A null constrains that force/energy model over the tested domain; it is not a proof covering all conceivable gravity theories.

**Nearest checked source / novelty:** Tesla's two specific patents NH10–NH11 and NASA's staged research framework NH21. This is an audit proposal, not a new propulsion mechanism or a claim that the patent establishes nanoparticle levitation. A modern optical/acoustic forward model must be supplied and reviewed before numerical execution.

## Scientific diagram briefs for implementation

All six plates should be vector diagrams with units and a “historical clue / modern model / measured result” distinction. A 3D image can illustrate a proposed apparatus, but must say **concept rendering, not a verified construction schematic**. Draw measured curves only from actual loop artifacts, and label synthetic curves accordingly.

### Plate D1 — Color inference and the missing variables

Use three panels rather than one mystical color wheel. Panel A: specimen parameters {radius distribution nm, shape, permittivity, medium index, concentration m⁻³} feed a named optical forward model. Panel B: illumination spectrum and detector response map that spectrum to recorded RGB; explicitly separate transmitted and reflected channels. Panel C: two parameter sets share one observable, then a calibrated independent assay splits them. Draw the duplicate channel beside it as a control that fails to split them. Include a small source strip “Newton 1718: conditional conjecture; Faraday 1857: alternative particle/mixing explanation; modern model: separately reviewed.” Do not put Newton's name on a Mie equation.

### Plate D2 — Matter and phase/state alternatives

Represent one fixed element budget as four differently bordered compartments: dispersed particles, clusters, dissolved species and deposited material. Arrow labels are **candidate processes**, not a recipe: association, dissociation, dissolution and settling. Show distinct phase/chemical-state tags on particles without changing the total element count; connect each tag to a required measurement (diffraction/speciation), rather than to an invented substance label. Underneath, use a diagnostic matrix: optical spectrum, entity count, dissolved fraction, recovered mass and phase signature. A color-only row remains ambiguous. Never draw a metallic tree growing into a proven “life force,” or label a historical green lion with a single molecule.

### Plate D3 — Number-weighted size → volume → count

Show equal-count 40/60 nm synthetic spheres with exact labels; their display area is decorative, not the calculation. Use two branches: average d then cube (125000 nm³), versus cube each d then average (140000 nm³). Both divide the same independently known mass concentration; show the resulting 1.12 count ratio. Add a monodisperse control. Keep diameter/volume/number units visible and name the population weighting. Cite NH17 as the existing result reproduced.

### Plate D4 — Cycle loss versus power

Pair a phase-lag sketch with two separate plots against x = 2πfτ. The first plots normalized per-cycle loss x/(1+x²); the second plots fixed-H power x²/(1+x²). Their vertical quantities must not share an unlabeled “resonance” axis. A lower band states assumptions and routes source power to stored field, sample heat and apparatus losses. This is magnetic material response, not an entire person's frequency.

### Plate D5 — Harmonics as measured rows

Draw an excitation spectrum, calibrated receive channels, correlated-noise block and system matrix. Color rows by independent information rather than frequency order; a duplicate harmonic row gets a distinct “repeated information” mark. An inset compares true rank/nullity with a regularized image. The patent's claimed benefit belongs in a dashed proposed-method box; only reviewed synthetic results get solid result boxes.

### Plate D6 — Supported particle and accounting boundary

Use a particle free-body panel, a separately drawn instrument/support recoil panel and an energy-flow boundary. Field, fluid and gravitational forces receive N labels; electrical/optical source arrows receive W, storage J. A source-off control removes a hypothesized support force. No arrows named “antigravity energy” appear until an independently defined and tested model supplies such a quantity. A photorealistic illustration must retain the conceptual-status caption.
