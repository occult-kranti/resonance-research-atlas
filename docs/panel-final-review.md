## Review methods

Round 1 used separately reconstructed energy/power columns, trapezoidal quadrature, an augmented matrix-exponential transient solution and a closed-form complex 2×2 inverse. Its independent quadrature closure was 2.780×10^-9 J and its exact transient displacement comparison stayed below 9.17×10^-11 m at sampled checkpoints.

Round 2 used rational PSD denominators, derivative sign checks and a separate three-point inverse with held-out frequencies. Round 3 used exact minors, a Leibniz determinant and a direct inverse instead of the producer's RREF routine, plus a continuous nonnegative-fiber proof. Round 4 used exact inverse-row norms and the completeness of error-cube corner extrema. Round 5 used exact inclusion–exclusion and the union bound, then independently recounted the saved 50,000 simulated studies.

Before reading the Round 6 producer results, the reviewer froze an alternate integer count of four-category target strings. It matched every one of the producer's 40 Fraction-DP prefix probabilities exactly, as well as all leakage powers. The saved 50,000-study archive hash and outcome counts also matched.

## Final dream/lucidity investigation

With four equally likely targets and 40 fixed trials, at least 16 matches pass the chosen one-sided nominal 5% test. The achieved null rejection probability is **2.6244884%**, because the count is discrete; 15 matches have tail probability about 5.4437%. Naively checking the same fixed-sample tail after every trial raises the chance of ever crossing a nominal threshold to **14.0373194%**. The repeated looks share outcomes, so their probability required a dependent sequential calculation.

A deliberate ordinary-information leakage model with lambda=0.1 raises the hit probability to 0.325 and the probability of passing the fixed cutoff to about 19.7807%. This is a disclosed positive control, not a discovered effect. It shows why successful prediction requires mechanism and leakage analysis in addition to a p-value.

The source packets distinguish retrospective reports, self-reported lucidity, physiology-supported lucidity, out-of-body phenomenology and external-target acquisition. The observational workflow uses the normal sleep schedule and locks a morning report before later interpretation. The website is a method/null simulator and journal aid. Public static client code cannot provide independent concealed-target custody; target/decryption material must remain unavailable to the participant before a locked response in any future empirical study.

## Novelty, remaining obligations and release boundary

The contribution is the integrated, source-bound workbench: explicit contracts, useful counterexamples, independent computational checks, repaired historical interpretations, diagrams, and honest links between evidence and hypotheses. The mathematical identities and statistical methods are established. No new physical law, effective therapy, free-energy device, transmutation, universal human frequency or metaphysical power was demonstrated.

Future empirical work would need calibrated sensors/assays, specified specimens or populations, uncertainties, data custody, feasible protocols and independent replication. The unread manuscripts, inaccessible records and unexecuted datasets remain leads.
