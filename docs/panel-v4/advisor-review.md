- R1: unstable reference and differential gain produce identical generated PCM; an independently constructed different-alpha rival also survives.
- R2: an out-of-budget gain nuisance excludes the true parameter even though the stated interval calculation is correct. Missing systematic bounds cannot be replaced with zero.
- R3: capacitor voltage gain near two, 0.2 apparent VA with zero capacitor mean real watts, and positive source-off load work all occur in a passive model. Initial storage and phase explain those readouts.
- R4: reversing current leaves the modeled induced magnetic force unchanged. Differentiating field energy at fixed current while omitting source work gives the wrong force sign and fails the budget. The complete support reaction cancels the internal force.
- R5: every individual injected-gravity readout is reproduced by an ordinary mass-proportional bias. This is a tested finite-fixture guard, not universal floating-point interval certification.
