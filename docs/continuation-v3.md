## Evidence rules

Every accepted computation binds its contract, units, input assumptions, code, output and review. Every source identifies actual reading depth and the claim it can support. Reddit and fringe discussions record claims and leads; a patent records a proposal; government custody records provenance. None alone establishes the claimed effect. A historical material name maps to a modern element only where the cited context supports that identification.

Scientific diagrams and 3D geometry are generated from explicit coordinates. Image-generation output is separately labeled concept art. A performable protocol is not an executed physical experiment. No human study, acoustic calibration, hardware result, transmutation, healing effect, new energy source or metaphysical connection is implied by software verification.

The [advisor review](panel-v3/advisor-review.md) summarizes each accepted finding and retained failure. The [detailed next-gate roadmap](panel-v3/next-gates.md) gives prerequisites, bounded actions, stop rules and claim limits for reproduction, physical recordings and future live-AI studies.
