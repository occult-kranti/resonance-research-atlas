## Machine schema

Each source has `id, title, url, date, version, sections_read, reading_depth, reading_status, provenance, claim_category, supports, does_not_support, unresolved, accessed, web_ref`. A null date means the exact source date was not established; `date_note` explains the available chronology. `web_ref` is retrieval provenance, not an extra source. Several reading depths are deliberately limited to abstracts or a catalogue.

Dictionary entries additionally identify `modernCandidate, elements, mappingStatus, primaryContext, contextLimit, sourceIds, discriminatingAssay, actualSampleAssayed`. A candidate element list must never render as an assayed composition.

## Sound: historical mechanism to a recorded file

[V3-FEYN49](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_49.html) motivates a reflection model. [V3-SMITH-COMB](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/pasp/Feedforward_Comb_Filters.html) provides its finite direct-plus-delay representation. [V3-FARINA2000](https://angelofarina.it/Public/Papers/134-AES00.PDF) provides a known-probe measurement route. These support a conditional model and total-transfer recovery, not a unique decomposition of unknown speaker, microphone and room responses.

P1 proposed a geometry intervention. P2 corrected its implicit assumption: if the device path is arbitrary and unknown, the full measured filter may be assigned to that path. A geometry interpretation therefore needs independent stability/calibration or a restricted model, and alternative factorizations must remain visible. This correction was sent to the advisor and acoustic producer before model production claims were admitted.

[V3-W3C-CAPTURE](https://www.w3.org/TR/mediacapture-streams/) motivates logging requested and reported processing settings, then checking actual performance. [V3-UPV2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.25947v1) is a measured-data follow-up with geometry/acquisition metadata. Its files were not downloaded, and no outside data are described as this project's measurements.

## Frequency and body claims: follow the exposure definition

The [r/audioengineering discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/audioengineering/comments/18dqjw8/432_hz_vs_440_hz/) cites the [432/440 pilot](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31031095/). Its abstract was read, and the linked 2020 corrigendum was noticed but not retrieved. Consequently no numerical efficacy conclusion is admitted from it here.

The [528 report](https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=87146) defines its exposure as retuned piano music. A follow-up would need the complete waveform, level, duration, order, expectation and a prespecified between-condition outcome. The mathematical fact that one condition's change is significant and another's is not does not establish a difference between conditions. These are hypotheses about perception/physiology, not evidence for a unique body frequency.

## Alchemy: name, material and measurement are distinct

## Jung, Pauli and Penrose: a useful question needs an observable

[Pauli's 1947–48 letters](https://www.ethistory.ethz.ch/texte/1947Pauli_Jung.pdf) were read as a four-page edited primary selection. Their interpretive questions motivate freezing temporal and semantic matching rules. They do not supply a controlled effect.

The proposed microtubule/EEG link is not an identifying bridge from ordinary audio beats to consciousness. Its detailed derivation and experimental literature remain unread leads in this pass.
