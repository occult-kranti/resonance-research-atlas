## Material identities for the plate and interface

The dictionary's conventional metal candidates are Au, Ag, Fe, Cu, Sn, Pb and Hg. They are conditional readings, not a universal chemical alphabet. Pb has a directly inspected lead/Saturn lexical link, accompanied by contrary contextual uses in the same index. The other conventional signs were checked in the editorial guide and require passage-level primary confirmation before a particular occurrence is decoded.

Antimony ore versus regulus, arsenic versus arsenic trioxide, and vitriol sulfate families illustrate why a name and a modern compound formula are different claims. The permitted panel display is “candidate interpretation; no historical sample assayed.” Material quantities, atomic number, oxidation state, phase, particle size and isotope ratios are separate properties. The inherited inverse-map results explain why an extra independent measurement can help, while a duplicate measurement may not.

## Panel recommendations and disagreement record

P1: start with the low-cost, known-probe total acoustic path. Sound provides inexpensive files, explicit units and repeatable counterexamples. The minimum modern model is a direct plus delayed path multiplied by the device response, with additive noise and a stated clock.

P2: the advisor challenged the initial geometry wording. Accepted correction: unknown device and room factors are not separately identifiable from their product. A microphone move may also change coupling or processing; only a controlled intervention with repeatability checks supports a geometric interpretation. Avoid labeling a fitted delay as a measured distance without the required mapping.

For a future bench protocol, preserve raw files, requested/reported microphone settings, source/receiver positions, sample rate, clock relation, gain, background recording and clipping/processing checks. Estimate repeatability before fixing the minimum detectable effect; no universal numerical pass threshold is supplied by the historical sources. Reject any result that depends on quietly excluding failed probes or adjusting the score after seeing the answer.

The historical lenses therefore contribute concrete practices: record alternative readings and failed mixtures; specify supply, phase and load; freeze interpretive rules; map geometry to equations; test the most damaging ordinary rival. They contribute no extra force, consciousness term or privileged frequency to the model.
