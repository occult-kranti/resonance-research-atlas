# Research program and parallel work map

This release completes a finite six-loop computational program. It does not complete all historical reading, perform a human trial or establish extraordinary physical effects. The final acceptance decisions and remaining gaps are in `panel-final-review.md` and `../research/panel-decisions.json`.

Source reading, coding and interface work were parallel. Numerical research loops were sequential: each new contract was selected after review of the previous loop. Parallelism did not substitute for that dependency.

## Six adaptive loops

1. **Tesla: source, storage and loss.** Derive a passive coupled-oscillator energy identity; independently compare transient and frequency-domain solutions. Residual energy does not become a claim of free energy.
2. **Tesla: measurement before interpretation.** Change source spectrum and readout while keeping poles fixed. Recover parameters in a positive control with known calibration. Keep the ideal spherical-shell reference separate from a real Earth–ionosphere solver.
3. **Newton: analysis and synthesis.** Construct multiple compositions with the same observed signs, then identify a genuinely independent measurement. The synthetic mixture is an inverse-problem counterexample, not a recovered alchemical recipe.
4. **Newton: useful measurement.** Perturb a nearly redundant assay and calculate exact bounded-noise amplification. Uniqueness and useful precision are separate requirements.
5. **Jung / chronology / Feynman controls.** Test the effect of searching a family of candidate matches. Preserve misses and compare independent and duplicate-test controls before discussing a selected pattern.
6. **Lucid dreaming and astral travel: final focused session.** Distinguish dream awareness, experienced self-location and external-information accuracy. Calibrate fixed-trial chance, ordinary information leakage and repeated checking; provide an ordinary-sleep observation journal and a blank separately controlled study plan. No participants or sleep recordings were analyzed.

## Reusable review contract

Apply the installed Newton analysis/synthesis, Tesla mechanism/resonance, historical physics panel and resonance research advisor skills. Each proposal records: source passage; status of the claim; mathematical variables and units; forward prediction; inverse identifiability; energy or information boundary; alternative explanations; distinguishing control; rejection condition; artifact path; independent review; next unresolved gap.

Named figures are documented research lenses, not impersonated experts or endorsements. The panel's independent agents share model-family provenance; human peer review and external replication remain open milestones.
