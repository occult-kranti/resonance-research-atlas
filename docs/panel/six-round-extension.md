# Prospective extension to six adaptive rounds

User steering extends the program from three to six rounds, with the sixth and final round focused on lucid dreaming and astral-travel claims. This supersedes the earlier three-round stop prospectively; it does not rewrite frozen contracts or count unexecuted work as complete.

| Round | Decision status at extension | Question | Why it follows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Executed and independently accepted | Does a passive coupled-resonator simulation close its energy budget? | Establish source, storage and loss accounting |
| 2 | Selected; contract under production | Do source and readout change a spectral peak while poles stay fixed? | Energy closure alone does not identify a measurement map |
| 3 | Conditional | Can distinct compositions yield the same visual/assay sign, and which independent observation distinguishes them? | Correct inherited unique-sign decipherment claims using an explicit inverse model |
| 4 | Conditional on Round 3 review | Does algebraic uniqueness survive noisy or nearly duplicate assays? | Full rank alone need not give a useful measurement |
| 5 | Conditional on Round 4 review | How often does trying many analyses manufacture an apparently striking result? | Well-conditioned measurements still permit biased selection |
| 6 | Required final topic; execution waits for Round 5 review | Separate dream/lucidity/body-location reports from blinded external-target information | Apply identification, calibration and selection controls to the requested dream/astral lane |

The next contract is chosen after predecessor review. The conditional questions may narrow or change if a failed control reveals a more important issue. Each round should have a few meaningful acceptance gates, not an inflated count of equivalent assertions.

## Provisional Round 4 design question

Keep the Round 3 synthetic mixture model. Move a third assay continuously from an exact duplicate toward an independent direction. Compare exact rank with the minimum singular value of the full square observation matrix, norm of the inverse, and recovery error under fixed bounded perturbations. Test an independent assay with the same declared noise budget. No chemical identities are assigned to synthetic components. If a noisy solver clips concentrations at zero, identify that as an added prior and compare it with the unconstrained result.

## Provisional Round 5 design question

Use a declared null model and compare one predeclared test with the minimum of multiple independent test p-values. An exact baseline is P(min p≤α)=1−(1−α)^m for continuous independent uniform p-values; a multiplicity correction has its own assumptions. Correlated tests must not silently use the independence equality. Save all trials, analysis choices and null outcomes. This prepares the final dream experiment to distinguish a prespecified success from a retrospectively chosen resemblance.

## Sixth-round design boundary

The sixth round comes last and does not run human subjects. It can complete a statistical measurement test, a reproducible null simulator, an observational session template and a source-bound experimental protocol. A simulation of successful guesses is not evidence of a human ability. An extraordinary mechanism would require empirical data, leakage controls, replication and competing explanations beyond this release.
