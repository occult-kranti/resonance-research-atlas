## What the sources actually support

Newton's *Experiments*, Add.3973, f.1r, records sublimation, weighed residues, separations, appearance, relative fusibility, and mixtures that failed to combine. The December 1678 entries are actual laboratory notes. They justify a workflow of explicit observations and negative results; they do not establish successful transmutation. [H01]

*Of Natures obvious laws & processes in vegetation*, Dibner MS.1031B, opens by comparing minerals with living growth and proposing a hidden principle. Its later passages also distinguish changes of texture from a proposed deeper process. This is a historical explanatory program, not evidence for an undiscovered modern force. [H02]

Newton's English Emerald Tablet manuscript is a transcription/translation of a Hermetic authority. The above/below and separation/recombination motifs can inspire a mapping question; they are not experimental laws derived by Newton. [H03]

In *Observations upon the Prophecies of Daniel*, I.II, Newton explicitly supplies a dictionary mapping natural images to political entities. That makes it a useful historical example of a declared interpretive dictionary. It is theological interpretation, not an instrument for measuring human frequencies. [H04]

## H-N1: frozen candidate contract for advisor selection

**Question.** Can two distinct synthetic material descriptions generate exactly the same measured channels, and which added observation distinguishes them?

**Model.** Let c=(c1,c2,c3) have nonnegative dimensionless relative amounts, not fractions constrained to sum to one. Define

```text
A = [[1,0,1], [0,1,1]]
c_left  = [0,0,1]
c_right = [1,1,0]
y_left = y_right = [1,1]
```

The nonnegative fiber is c(t)=(1−t,1−t,t), 0≤t≤1. Its direction (−1,−1,1) lies in the kernel of A. This is an exact algebraic result. The word spectrum is only an analogy here: there are two synthetic normalized measurement channels, with no claim of real optical calibration.

**Positive discriminator.** Append a3=[1,1,0]. The extended square matrix has determinant −2, hence full rank. The candidate outputs in the new channel are 0 and 2.

**Damaging control.** Append a3_duplicate=[2,0,2]. It is twice the first row; rank stays two and ambiguity remains. A successful fitting routine cannot create missing information.

**Acceptance tests.** Exact symbolic products and determinant agree; numerical residual for the indistinguishable pair is zero within declared arithmetic tolerance; duplicate channel stays rank deficient; independent assay resolves the noise-free pair. Add controlled synthetic noise only after this algebra is frozen. Report sensitivity to noise through the singular values rather than reporting a universal certainty.

**Scope.** This proves only the behavior of the displayed observation map. It does not identify Newton's compounds, show an object's entire frequency, or establish the completeness of any real assay. More realistic spectroscopy needs reference spectra, mixing validity, sample/path-length assumptions, calibration, and uncertainty.

## Newton's 2060 calculation: context, not a physics forecast

Yahuda MS.7.3g, THEM00437, f.13v (with its insertion), gives the arithmetic 800+1260=2060 while interpreting prophetic days as years. Newton presents this as a conditional earliest date in that interpretation, allows a later end, and immediately says his purpose is to restrain rash predictions. Calling it a certain prediction that the physical Earth ends in 2060 changes the source's meaning. [H05]

Website card: title “Why 2060 appears in Newton's manuscript”; show the three layers **textual symbol → selected chronology → arithmetic**. No countdown widget. A safe computational extension studies how many historical dates a flexible symbol-to-date dictionary can fit. Freeze candidate dates, scoring, and a held-out corpus before testing. This investigates interpretive flexibility, not the truth of prophecy.
