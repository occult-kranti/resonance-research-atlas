# Nanoparticles and frequency: five additional research rounds

This program extends the original six rounds; it does not replace the final lucid-dreaming session. It is a selected source review and a set of reproducible synthetic experiments, with proposed laboratory follow-ups. Acceptance and execution states come from [the decision ledger](../research/nanoparticle-panel-decisions.json).

## Five-round dependency chain

1. **N1 — Which frequency maximum?** Separate magnetic energy per cycle from average power. Establish peak/RMS conventions and a passive energy budget. Extension: examine whether apparent relaxation time stays constant across a calibrated spectrum.
2. **N2 — What does the thermometer identify?** Account for heat leakage and sensor lag. A separately metered calibration and held-out pulse duration test whether apparent changes are instrument effects. Retain the exact scale ambiguity in power, heat capacity and conductance.
3. **N3 — Which mechanism survives another measurement?** Compare a single-time response with a positive mixture, using held-out frequencies and controlled viscosity. One successful fit does not uniquely reveal microscopic cause.
4. **N4 — Response weights are not particle counts.** The preceding round exposed a population-weighting ambiguity. Compare the exact number-weighted volume moment with a mean-diameter estimate, then test conditional core-size magnetic weighting at held-out frequencies.
5. **N5 — A beat depends on the measurement channel.** N4 requires a calibrated physical drive before its material response can be applied. Analyze actual OpenSync WAV exports through a linear channel and a quadratic detector. Use equal-RMS and phase-reversal controls; retain the missing conversion from digital samples to a physical field.

This document records the dependency policy; the finalized per-round contracts and decision ledger take precedence over summaries above. No precomputed batch is presented as five adaptive deliberations.

## Experiment admission standard

Every proposal needs a stated field or input, observable and unit, competing mechanism, identifiable parameters, reference calibration, source-off control, held-out test, and a result that would defeat its explanation. Numerical results must name the model and never acquire an empirical status merely because a solver converges. Frequency in hertz is a rate; equal rates across sound, magnetic excitation and optical radiation do not establish equal coupling.

Novelty is scoped to new project hypotheses, counterexamples and integrated controls. The checked prior art is linked. No exhaustive novelty search or first-discovery claim is made.

## Next physical work

Choose one sealed inert reference experiment from [hardware concepts](panel-nanoparticles/hardware-concepts.md). Begin with calibration, not a biological endpoint. Fix the sample distribution and concentration basis, independently characterize the instrument response, and preregister uncertainty and exclusions. Preserve null observations and residuals. Any subsequent human-benefit study would be a separate research program; no treatment effect is established here.

The open-tool register records OOMMF, k-Wave, miepython and metrology resources as inspected, unrun, or partially accessed. Their availability is not a claim they have been installed or validated for this project. Current numerical work uses pinned Python libraries and the repository's existing deterministic audio engine.
