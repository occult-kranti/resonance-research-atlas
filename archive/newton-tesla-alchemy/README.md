## Epistemic Key

- **[ESTABLISHED]** — mainstream science or scholarship with solid documentation or consensus.
- **[HISTORICAL]** — documented primary sources: manuscripts, patents, letters, dated interviews, declassified files, real programs and events — regardless of whether their claims were true.
- **[FRINGE]** — a fringe or occult claim, community, or source reported accurately but **without endorsement**.
- **[SPECULATIVE]** — a hypothesis or interpretation, including plausible-but-unproven readings and unbuilt designs.

## Disclaimer

This archive is compiled for research and educational purposes only. Fringe claims are documented and mapped as claims — they are not endorsed. Where a quotation or attribution could not be verified against a primary source, it is flagged inline in the dossiers themselves.
