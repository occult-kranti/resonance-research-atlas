02 / physical protocol

A recording needs a geometry.

Proposed apparatus, not an executed measurement. The distance, device settings, and room remain part of the data.

Nominal geometry in metres · proposed, unbuilt · no measured delayDownload setup SVG ↓

Minimum kit

  • One ordinary small speaker and a playback source
  • A phone able to save an original recording
  • A stiff card, books or cardboard positioning jigs, and tape measure
  • A quiet room and a way to note device settings

One controlled comparison

  1. Place source and microphone 0.50 m apart at 0.75 m height in the proposed plan; measure actual coordinates and orientation.
  2. Record source-off background and a reference with the card absent. Log phone app, gain mode, volume and file format.
  3. Put the card at a documented position; record the same known playback at a comfortable low level, then repeat without changing source or phone.
  4. Move only the card, record again, and retain all original files and settings. A changed digital trace is a whole-chain observation.

Interpretation limit. Automatic gain control, compression, speaker response, phone filtering, noise, and room reflections can mimic a changed response. Uncalibrated phone levels cannot establish absolute sound pressure or transfer gain.
