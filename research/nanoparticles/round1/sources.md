# N1 source record

**ROS-2002:** Ronald E. Rosensweig, “Heating magnetic fluid with alternating
magnetic field,” *Journal of Magnetism and Magnetic Materials*252 (2002),370–374.
DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-8853(02)00706-0 . Primary full-text copy inspected
by the source researcher and advisor, §§2–4; not raw experimental data reanalysis.

The adopted conventions are a peak sinusoidal H amplitude, a volume susceptibility,
single relaxation time, weak-field linear response and an absorbed power per unit
volume. The single-Debye susceptibility and loss expression are established theory.
The project contribution is an inspectable comparison of different optimization
questions and a passive-budget benchmark, not invention of those equations.

The full synthetic x range is not a source-backed material validity range. Real
particle size, anisotropy, interactions, hydrodynamic volume, temperature and
immobilization would require additional characterization. The source's more
detailed Néel expression must not be replaced silently by an unqualified bare
Arrhenius expression. No such material inversion is attempted in N1.

The result hashes this local source record. It does not claim to bind the bytes of
the external publication, which is identified by its DOI and reading scope.

The page range was corrected after the first run; see
[bibliographic-erratum.md](bibliographic-erratum.md). The original frozen contract
and original result metadata remain preserved.
