# N2 source and derivation boundary

**NANO-SKIN2025:** Skinner et al., “An All-in-One Nanoheater and Optical Thermometer
Fabricated from Fractal Nanoparticle Assemblies,” *ACS Nano*19(2025),13779–13789.
DOI https://doi.org/10.1021/acsnano.4c16452 . Primary author PDF:
https://www.np.phy.cam.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/sites/50/2025/04/ACSNano25_SERSnanoheater.pdf .

Source researcher read photothermal properties, combined heating/SERS discussion
and Methods on p8 (including a visual check of Eqs6–8); advisor independently
inspected the methods/equation layout. These are optical/plasmonic heating methods,
not empirical validation of N1's magnetic model. Well-mixed calorimetry, blank
comparison and laser-off cooling motivate the measurement-design questions.

The N2 heat equation follows directly from an assumed lumped first law. Its
first-order thermometer lag is an explicit project surrogate, not a measured
sensor law extracted from this paper. No published efficiency or dataset is
claimed reproduced. A preliminary OCR concern about Eq6 units was resolved by
visual inspection; the source is not alleged to contain that error.
