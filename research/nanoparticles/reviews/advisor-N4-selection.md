# N4 selected after N3 admission

N3 discriminated two synthetic response models at held-out frequencies, but a susceptibility amplitude is not a particle count. Select the missing normalization question. This improves the old single-peak inference by requiring independent mass and size observations before interpreting a spectral mixture.

The advisor read Farkas et al. (2025), DOI 10.1021/acs.analchem.4c05990, the NIST-hosted accepted paper's abstract and PNC derivation/current-practice sections, PDF page 4, with the finite size-moment correction discussed on page 7. URL: https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=958200 . The paper establishes use of arithmetic mean particle volume, not the volume calculated from mean diameter, under known density and a number-weighted size distribution. This is a contemporary measurement correction reproduced here, not a new result attributed to this panel. It does not validate our hypothetical magnetic material.

For number fractions p_i, core volumes V_i, common density ρ and mass concentration c_m, number concentration is n=c_m/[ρΣp_iV_i]. Synthetic equal-number core diameters 40 and 60 nm have E[d³]=140000 nm³ while E[d]³=125000 nm³; the naive estimate is 12% larger. Use synthetic ρ=5000 kg/m³ and c_m=0.001 kg/m³. Fractions and moment ratios admit exact arithmetic.

Rosensweig's equilibrium weak-field expression χ_i=μ0φM_d²V_core/(3kBT) combined with φ_i=n_iV_core gives χ0∝Σn_iV_core,i². Only under dilute identical-material equilibrium assumptions are susceptibility weights proportional to number times d_core^6. Thus the same hypothetical equal-count pair has number weights (1/2,1/2), mass weights (8/35,27/35), and magnetic response weights (64/793,729/793).

Keep core and hydrodynamic diameters separate. Set hydrodynamic diameters to 50 and 70 nm for a conditional Brownian locked-moment normalized-response example, with T=298 K and η=0.001 Pa s. No Néel process, coating structure, anisotropy, material validity or absolute susceptibility is identified. The chosen 40–60 nm cores are mathematical parameters, not a claim that an arbitrary material at these sizes satisfies the model.

Project hypothesis NP-H4: after independent mass and number-weighted size characterization, physically appropriate response weights predict held-out normalized spectral shape better than number weights in this model. Both candidate curves share the same DC normalization; held-out frequencies reveal the difference. A monodisperse control removes both count and weighting biases, while label exchange leaves predictions invariant.

The improved future experiment would use characterized sealed standards, measure mass concentration and a number-weighted core-size distribution independently, determine hydrodynamic sizes separately, and compare spectra at frequencies withheld from calibration. Agglomeration, size-dependent magnetization, interactions, saturation and sample preparation are rivals that can defeat the simplified mapping. Color alone cannot supply these quantities; Faraday's historical mixture-versus-state alternatives and Newton's conditional optical arguments motivate additional observables, not a nanoparticle identification.
